# QuantaTale brand guide

## Core identity

- **Brand:** QuantaTale
- **Tagline:** KNOW MORE. SEE DIFFERENTLY.
- **Category:** Evidence-led educational explainers and fact-based animated videos.
- **Purpose:** Turn trustworthy information about the world into clear, memorable and visually immersive stories.
- **Audience:** Curious global viewers; content spans beginner-friendly explainers through deeper educational topics.
- **Voice:** Curious, intelligent, confident, accessible and honest. Never sensationalize uncertain findings.

## Channel description

QuantaTale turns fascinating facts, scientific discoveries and complex ideas into immersive visual explanations. Explore science, technology, history, nature, space, engineering, psychology and the surprising details of everyday life through concise narration, original code-generated animation and cinematic holographic visuals. We put evidence before hype and make difficult ideas easier to understand.

## Short description

Fascinating facts and complex ideas, explained through cinematic holographic animation. Know more. See differently.

## Visual system

- **Primary background:** midnight navy / near-black.
- **Primary light:** electric cyan holographic lines and highlights.
- **Secondary accent:** restrained violet for depth and contrast.
- **Typography:** clean geometric sans-serif; bold, high-legibility titles; short on-screen phrases.
- **Symbol direction:** an original geometric QT monogram or a quantum-inspired portal/beam motif. Keep it simple enough to read as a small channel avatar.
- **Motion:** precise line reveals, scanning grids, particles, dimensional diagrams, luminous transitions and controlled camera movement.
- **Composition:** narration-led; every visual should clarify the current idea, not merely decorate it.

## Editorial rules

1. Prioritize primary sources, scientific institutions, universities, government agencies and reputable publishers.
2. Preserve source URLs, dates, claim wording and relevant context.
3. Seek independent corroboration for important claims.
4. Show uncertainty and disagreement rather than hiding them.
5. Never present automated extraction or scoring as complete fact verification.
6. Require human editorial approval before public release.
7. Use strong hooks without distorting evidence or overstating conclusions.

## Platform formats

- Vertical 9:16 for YouTube Shorts and TikTok.
- Horizontal 16:9 for longer educational videos.
- Keep captions readable on mobile and keep key text inside platform-safe areas.

## Naming and compatibility

QuantaTale is the public-facing product and channel name. The existing Python import namespace `factflow` and legacy `factflow` command are retained temporarily to avoid breaking current users and scripts. New installations expose the `quantatale` command. The GitHub repository slug remains `Factflow` until its rename can be performed through repository administration settings.
